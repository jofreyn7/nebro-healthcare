— How we started

Who we are

Nebro Company Limited is a fast-growing pharmaceutical and medical goods supply company in Tanzania, dedicated to providing high-quality medicines and healthcare products to communities in need. Established in 2024, Nebro Company has quickly become a trusted name in the industry, with a strong presence across both Tanzania and the East Africa region.

Registered under incorporation certificate No. 181607947, with valid Business License No. BL01396912024-2500025470 and TIN 181-607-947, Nebro Company is fully compliant with Tanzanian regulations and Tanzania Food and Drugs Authority (TFDA) standards — a commitment to transparency that has earned the trust of customers, partners, and regulators alike.

— Our Expertise

Our Comprehensive Medical Capabilities

Medical Equipment & Supplies Procurement

We source, deliver and verify premium medical hardware and consumables from verified international manufacturers. From frontline clinical instruments to complex ICU monitors, we provide tools built for accuracy.

Certified Installation & Commissioning

Improper deployment damages expensive equipment. Our resident engineers handle physical installation, rigorous electrical safety testing, software setup, and hardware calibration to ensure perfect day-one compliance.

Preventive & Corrective Maintenance

Protect your investment with custom service contracts. We conduct detailed functional checks, software updates, and component replacements, alongside rapid on-site emergency repair during unexpected system failures.
